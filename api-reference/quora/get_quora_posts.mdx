Search public Quora Space posts by keyword `query`, with `limit` 1–10 (default 10) and native `time` controls `all`, `hour`, `day`, `week`, `month` and `year`. Native post search can also return personal-profile posts: only Space-hosted URLs with a native Space name are returned here. Unknown associations are counted in `unconfirmed_space_rows_omitted`, without fetching extra rows to fill the window.

Items contain URL, nullable title/search text, author name/link, upvotes/comments/shares and native publish timestamp. Source query/type/time traces and duplicated URL/title/text/author/count/date fields must agree. Post and author numeric IDs remain null; none is inferred from slugs. Missing bodies stay null; a supplied search body may be a snippet or long text, with no full-post completeness guarantee. Source Space association, public values and counter precision are not independently verified. No profile, post-detail, media or asset fetch occurs.

`since` and `until` apply inclusively to this bounded window. A calendar lower date starts at midnight UTC; a calendar upper date includes the whole UTC day through its final microsecond. ISO timestamps require a timezone and accept up to six fractional digits. Original publish precision is preserved; no millisecond truncation can incorrectly include a later timestamp at an upper bound. Undated confirmed Space posts stay visible. There is no refill, history walk or chronological-order claim.

`published_after`, `start_date` and `from_date` alias `since`; `published_before`, `end_date` and `to_date` alias `until`. Multiple absolute aliases must denote the same instant. `publish_time` accepts rolling `yesterday`, `this-week`, `this-month`, `last-3-months` and `last-6-months` lower bounds (1/7/30/90/180 days). `max_age_days` accepts 0–36500 days, including decimals. Lower bounds intersect. Relative filtering uses the response's immutable `observed_at` clock, exposed in `date_range`, so receipt reconstruction and replay do not drift.

A nonempty **source** window settles five customer credits from a five-credit hold, even when association/date filtering removes every row. A completed empty source window settles zero and refunds the hold without proving global absence. `source_count`, `returned_count`, `filtered_out`, `date_filtered_out` and association omissions describe that distinction. Source failure, contradictory fields, duplicate URLs and overflow cannot settle a successful charge. Confirmed failures, cancellation (499) and deadline expiry (504) fully refund; source authentication/quota errors return 503. Immutable idempotent replay performs no source work and adds no charge.

One pinned Apify `fatihtahta/quora-scraper` build `0.8.14` run uses 512 MiB, a 40-second Actor timeout and a 45-second active window covering reservation, transport/body reads and projection. Source and output caps are 2 MiB each; optional body text is bounded at 50,000 characters. No automatic retry, pagination, enrichment or filter refill occurs. Already accepted remote work may finish under its original cap after local cancellation.

The provider requires a **$1 maximum-charge setting** even for small runs; requests save at most ten records at published $0.00099/outputRecord pricing. Only exact pinned, single-keyword Profile/Topic/Space/Post plans qualify under their verified row, memory, byte and deadline bounds; other Actor caps stay unchanged. Reported usage is an incomplete estimate, separate from customer credits. The shared Apify journal reserves $1 of its $5 ceiling for each attempted source window, regardless of the lower eventual usage estimate.
