Supply `query`, optional `company_size` and comma-separated numeric `industry_ids`, then `page=1–20` or an opaque `cursor`. `has_jobs` and `geocode` remain unsupported. Size bands match the returned About range exactly. Industry matching uses the exact reported IDs, without expanding descendants. Unknown or mismatched fields are excluded explicitly; this does not claim complete native filter membership or exhaustive discovery.

A source page holds up to fifty records and can contain school pages or records outside the requested filters. The response returns companies/showcases only. `source_count`, `filtered_out` and `filter_counts` explain the source window, including `previously_seen`, `non_company`, `company_size_mismatch` and `industry_mismatch`. `source_position` retains the original source position after exclusions. Source totals and ordering can change between calls and are not an exact count/rank for the filtered result set.

Each fresh request holds ten credits. A nonempty returned company page settles ten; a filtered-empty or all-repeat page settles zero and refunds the whole hold. An empty source dataset or source error remains an unbilled failure, not confirmed global absence. A filtered-empty source page may still have a continuation when it observed new identities.

Signed `lcs3_` cursors bind the original query/filters, retain every observed source ID once and keep the original one-hour expiry. Their history includes school/filter-excluded records. Cross-page repeats are omitted without refilling; a full source page with at least one new identity may continue, while an all-repeat page stops. No identity is discarded to fit the 16,000-character cursor bound; exceeding it stops explicitly. `lcs1_` and `lcs2_` cursors must restart. A direct page begins a new chain and cannot check unvisited pages. The source exposes at most twenty pages/1,000 accessible records even when its reported total is larger; `complete` remains false.

Native company slugs containing `&` or `.` retain their exact identity. Bare website-domain labels appear in `author.ext.source_website_domain` with `external_url=null`; no HTTP(S) scheme is invented. Full website and logo URLs are validated metadata and are never fetched. Unknown counts, vacancy facts and precision remain unknown.
