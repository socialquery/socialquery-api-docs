Supply a plain-text `name`, optional `type=person|company` (default person), and distinct comma-separated `platforms` from LinkedIn, Instagram, X (`twitter`) and YouTube; default all four. One name-based index query examines at most ten hits and retains up to eight unique canonical direct-profile URLs. `candidates` limits the total returned across platforms to 1–5 (default three). It does not guarantee each platform is represented. YouTube supports ASCII handles here; channel IDs, Unicode handles, other platforms, hydration and continuation remain gaps.

Optional `employer`, `location` and bare-domain `website` are model context only. They do not constrain the search or establish verified associations. Index titles/snippets are bounded observations, not current profile facts. Native platform IDs and confirmed identity remain unknown; an empty candidate sample never proves an account is absent.

One bounded xAI Chat call assesses the candidate titles/snippets. Every non-uncertain label requires exact supporting quotes; invented IDs, added candidates or ungrounded quotes reject the assessment. Scores are uncalibrated ordinal fit judgments, not probabilities or verification of identity/ownership. Missing hints are not contradictions. No model browsing, tools, contact lookup or inferred sensitive traits are used. Internal reasoning content is omitted.

Hold two credits. A nonempty fully assessed sample settles two; missing/failed assessment or no candidate settles zero. On unavailable assessment, candidates remain ordered by source position with null judgments and match ranks. Search and model failures never establish identity or absence. Failed paid model work may still have upstream cost; reported cost is retained, while unknown cost stays null.
