Each Korean presence index is the retained distinct URL count divided by requested display. The Korean mean requires all six corpora to succeed. Global presence divides retained rows by ten slots per requested source and requires every source to complete. Difference points are `100 * (Korean index - global index)`, a declared local sampling heuristic. Missing coverage stays null. Search totals do not become Korean volume, and raw relevance is not proof of topic identity or population-level over-indexing.

`date_from` and `date_to` are inclusive UTC calendar days, applied locally to collected relevance windows. Unknown dates are excluded and counted only when dates are requested. Several corpora have no dates; this can remove most rows and is not evidence of absence or historical retrieval.
