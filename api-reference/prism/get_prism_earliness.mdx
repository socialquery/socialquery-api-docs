Retrieves bounded topic observations across Google Trends monthly keyword history, TikTok/X/Reddit/YouTube/Instagram search, and TikTok/Meta ad libraries. term is one non-URL keyword (up to 200 characters, no comma comparisons); region defaults to US and accepts uppercase country or source geographic subdivision codes. Google receives the full code, TikTok search/Meta receive its country, YouTube receives its country as video viewability market, and other sources have no documented geographic filter. Codes and source coverage are not independently validated geographic availability, author location or verified topic identity. An unsupported source country remains unavailable rather than silently falling back.

Each social/ad lane makes one fixed query and retains at most ten records, with no deeper cursor walk, media/profile/detail enrichment or fallback. TikTok requests all-time/date-posted, Reddit all/new, YouTube newest videos, Instagram one reel-search page, X its documented query, Meta keyword-exact-phrase/all-status/relevance and TikTok public library its documented query only. Native rankings differ; none proves oldest-first or complete history. IDs, native URL namespaces, privacy exclusions and dates are checked. Text is a Unicode-safe prefix of at most 1,000 UTF-8 bytes; local_text_truncated records local truncation, while native text completeness remains unverified. Zero epoch placeholders are unknown. Delivered dates are validated between 2004 and source capture; older/impossible/future timestamps make that source unavailable. Ad start observations remain whole UTC-day intervals, not exact impression instants. TikTok library rows expose advertiser metadata without documented creative text, so relevant ad presence stays unknown; Creative Center details use a different ID namespace and are not substituted. Meta title/body text may be classified but native is_active does not verify current delivery. Unavailable/unclassified results never prove absence; explicit empty windows describe only that window.

One optional existing xAI Chat request judges at most 25 round-robin 450-byte content prefixes with a 14k-byte sample envelope and at least three texts. keep requires an exact quote from the same indexed prefix; drop/uncertain/unclassified records cannot establish a first relevant observation. Earliest matched social timestamps are the earliest among delivered, classified and dated records. earliest_global_censored=true and index_start_reached=null retain the unobserved-history limit. first_ever_post_at and earliness_score stay null. Model failure leaves observations available with unknown relevance and the same source price.

Google Trends preserves every returned calendar month from January 2004 through the source creation month, normalized integer indices, reported partial flags and monthly intervals. Reported <1 remains observed interest even when extracted index is zero; first_reported_interest_month is distinct from first_positive_integer_month. Zero never proves absence. Google does not verify keyword entity equivalence. A Google-minus-social gap is only an outward-rounded range between reported monthly and timestamp intervals across different populations, without real-world lead or causality claims. No exact first-search date, complete history, global origin, whole-library absence or late/early verdict is inferred.
