<Note>Implemented locally and disabled until combined source, capacity and settlement verification. Unavailable requests return HTTP 503 without consuming credits.</Note>

Read up to four consumer-demand inputs in parallel: app-review sample rates, the slope of indexed web mentions, newest-first Reddit post sample rates, and an Amazon product's written-review count. Supply store IDs to include app reviews. Amazon resolves the first organic search result; that ranking is not an entity-identity judgment. Its rating-vote count is returned separately and never stands in for written reviews.

Every index input is explicit. The declared local weights are 0.40 / 0.25 / 0.20 / 0.15 for app reviews / mentions / Reddit / commerce, with respective anchors of 10 reviews per day / 100 indexed mentions per observed day / 10 posts per day / 1,000 written reviews. Each known level is capped at 100 and the weighted mean re-normalizes over known requested axes. Missing inputs stay null and reduce component coverage. These are heuristic anchors, not purchase probabilities or claims of an identical competitor score.

App and Reddit rates divide the dated sample size by its observed exposure in days, with a one-day minimum. Samples are bounded and are not complete histories. Only the mention index has a stateless historical slope: ordinary least squares uses observed UTC daily points and never fills missing days with zero. App, Reddit and commerce week-over-week changes remain unavailable without repeated observations. Dates apply to the mention axis only.

The hold is 30 credits. Strict-majority selected-source failure charges 15. No completed source means a failed request and full refund. A successful empty source retains its coverage charge while its unknown demand metric stays null. Optional countries currently supported: US, GB, DE, FR, CA, AU and JP. Existing provider budgets and the unallocated commerce task gate remain in force.
