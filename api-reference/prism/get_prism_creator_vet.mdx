Public creator observation windows on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. Supply platform and a bare handle or canonical profile URL. depth is 1–25 (default10); deep selects at most25 posts, three comment windows of20 and at most three commenter profiles. Standard selects at most the requested depth and two comment windows of10. These are upper bounds over one documented own-post window, without a history walk or global latest/ranking guarantee. include defaults to engagement,posting; an empty string omits those computations. cross_platform adds three additional same-handle profile lookups among TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and X. Shared handles never verify ownership or a person's identity.

Profile and post identities are checked before composition. Instagram comment parents require an independent metadata numeric-ID/shortcode join; TikTok/YouTube URL IDs must match the feed. Each post reads one native comment page, without replies. Local top-by-known-likes selection applies only within that scanned page; YouTube's source page is time ordered and other sources retain their native order. At most three distinct native commenter IDs with supported lookup handles receive public profiles; mismatched/private/missing profiles remain unknown. Known zero followers or post counters are descriptive observations, not fake/bot or low-quality classifications. Unknown counters stay out of the eligible denominator. Engagement ratios require known counters and positive views; posting intervals cover dated delivered rows, not global activity or independent people.

Research reads one indexed news window (five rows), one Reddit query window and one first Hacker News window (20 hits, at most five displayed by observed points). Query is the literal handle plus controversy, a retrieval input rather than evidence of controversy or identity. Titles/snippets, native IDs, source positions and validated dates remain bounded observations; URLs are inert metadata. Source absence/failure never proves an absence of issues. No media, links, article bodies, contact details or extra detail crawl are fetched.

One optional existing xAI Chat request judges at most six comment and six research prefixes, round-robin by post/source, 700 UTF-8 bytes each. Labels require exact indexed same-prefix quotes. Generic comment shares require at least three judged comments and are text judgments, not bot labels. Repeated-text share uses locally untruncated delivered texts, without claiming original text completeness. Research flags require a text hypothesis matching the creator and blaming them; namesake, victim and uncertain rows are excluded. Reported legal/platform/commercial/interpersonal/other issue categories and ordinal severity1–3 describe supplied text only. Counts and highest observed severity are computed in code. Identity, allegation truth, completeness and calibrated probabilities remain unverified.
