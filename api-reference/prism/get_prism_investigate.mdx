---
title: "Prism Investigate"
openapi: "GET /prism/investigate"
---

<Note>Implemented locally and disabled pending source capacity and combined verification. Unavailable calls return HTTP 503 without consuming credits.</Note>

Pass a plain-text `goal` (1–500 characters), `budget` (1–200; default 40), and `max_steps` (1–12; default 8). The walker chooses among TikTok video search, Instagram native account search, indexed Instagram reel search, and public profiles or post comments found earlier. It cannot invent a follow-up handle or URL, repeat an attempted operation, contact anyone, or perform writes.

The result contains `trail`, `results`, `stopped_because`, `credits_used`, and `credits_refunded`. Each trail step records its exact operation, source scope, outcome and charge. A failed step preserves earlier evidence. Stop reasons are `goal_met`, `budget`, `step_limit`, and `blocked`. At the read cap a final model decision may finish, but cannot fetch another page.

`goal_met` requires a search, a successful follow-up, a model confidence of at least 0.8, and checked verbatim excerpts including follow-up evidence. It remains a model assessment for your review, not proof of complete or accurate research. No inferred lead labels are added. An empty comment page does not prove the post has no comments.

The request holds the complete budget. Usable search/profile pages cost 1 credit; nonempty TikTok comment pages cost 1 and Instagram comment pages cost 5. Failed reads, empty comment pages, and model decisions are free. Unused credits are refunded. Set `X-Max-Credits` to cover the hold, not an estimated final charge. Responses are not cached; the public playground uses samples only.

Collection is limited to first pages and a 55-second total deadline. Text is capped at 1,200 characters per row; the planner sees at most 80 recent rows and 200 affordable observed candidates. Instagram native search supplies accounts; indexed reel search is explicitly separate. These samples are not exhaustive platform searches. Source fields such as avatars, external contact fields and raw payloads are excluded from planner input.
