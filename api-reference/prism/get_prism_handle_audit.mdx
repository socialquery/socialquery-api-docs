<Note>This partial Bluesky-only audit is staged locally and disabled. Production remains on release 2026-10-11.120. A successful native profile/feed check does not establish complete reference parity or production readiness.</Note>

Select `platforms=bluesky` explicitly and supply one public Bluesky DNS handle, leading `@`, `did:plc` identifier or canonical profile URL. `sample` requests 1–25 author-feed rows, default 10. The feed is bound to the resolved DID, requests `posts_no_replies` with `includePins=false`, and still validates ownership and excludes reposts, pinned items and replies locally. It never refills or follows a cursor. Other platforms, relevance analysis and stale-if-error cache evidence are unsupported.

The local score version is `socialquery-bluesky-sample-1.0.0`. It differs from reference score model 2.0.0. Five components are reported: presence 100 for a usable public profile; audience `min(100,20*log10(1+followers))`; engagement `min(100,20*median((likes+replies+reposts+quotes)/followers*100))`; activity `max(0,100-days_since_newest_sampled_post*100/90)`; and richness `50*(known_media_fraction+mean(min(text_codepoints,300)/300))`. The weights are 0.10, 0.25, 0.25, 0.25 and 0.15 respectively. Values round to four decimals.

At least three eligible originals are required for activity/text/media measures and three complete-counter posts for engagement. Zero or unknown follower counts leave engagement unknown. An overall score requires every component; missing evidence produces `score: null` and `insufficient_evidence`. Otherwise scores of at least 70, at least 40, and below 40 produce `promising_sample`, `review_sample`, and `limited_sample`. These are advisory sample heuristics, never calibrated probabilities or a measure of audience authenticity. A fingerprint binds the local version, normalized request, evidence capture times, reference time and returned source observations.

The newest sampled post is not established as the account’s globally latest activity. Source post totals and badge flags are observations; trusted-verifier status does not become a verified badge. No cross-platform ownership, full-history comment count, campaign performance or pull-cost estimate is inferred. Original profile names/bios, post prose, media/links, reply targets and reposter enrichment are omitted from both output and independent receipts. Media presence uses declared embed kinds and does not prove playback or availability.

A usable profile holds/settles five credits even when its feed is empty, unavailable or too small for scoring. Unknown feed evidence stays distinct from an explicit successful empty feed. Profile failures fail unbilled and do not prove absence. One profile lookup and one feed attempt use the public Bluesky API without credentials, media downloads or inference. Both share a 30-second deadline including reservation; each response is bounded to 2 MiB, canonical output to 1 MiB, and receipts expire after ten minutes. Runtime capacity is zero/unallocated with all activation flags false. Wider native empty/error, other-platform, reference scoring/field and combined REST/MCP/Python/ledger acceptance remains pending.
