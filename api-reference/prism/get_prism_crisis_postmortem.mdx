title: "Prism Crisis Postmortem"
openapi: "GET /prism/crisis-postmortem"
---

<Note>Implemented locally and disabled pending source capacity and combined verification. Unavailable calls return HTTP 503 without consuming credits.</Note>

Reconstructs a bounded chronology from indexed anger-ranked citations, newest Reddit posts, date-filtered Hacker News stories and native TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and X search pages. Supply a `brand` and optional UTC window (default: 30 days ago through today, at most 366 calendar days). Up to five comma-separated `crisis_terms` can narrow the incident. Native content-analysis filters and local social-text matching are explicitly distinguished.

Every event has `time_basis`: native, reported publication or indexed. A crawl timestamp cannot establish who published first. Only native timestamps enter the earliest-observed origin candidates and platform-order calculation. Ties remain visible; `actual_origin_verified` is always false. Chronology is not proof of causality, identity or an allegation.

The peak lists every tied maximum observed indexed-volume day. Missing daily buckets remain unknown. The report exposes sample sizes and dropped rows. URL duplicates prefer native provenance while retaining all source observations.

Narrative is on by default and uses at most 20 bounded excerpts with verified IDs and exact quotations. Pass `include=` for the raw timeline. Model failure preserves raw evidence.

The request holds 35 credits. Five billing sources are counted: volume curve, anger search, Reddit, HN and cross-platform spread. At least one completed spread platform makes that source available. Fewer than three completed sources charges **18 credits and refunds 17**. Valid empty sources count as completed. All-source failure receives the normal full refund.
