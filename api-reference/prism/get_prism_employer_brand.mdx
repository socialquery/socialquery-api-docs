Retrieves bounded public workplace-discussion windows for a company. Default sources are Reddit, YouTube, indexed news, and Naver blog/cafe search. An optional canonical linkedin_url adds that company's public posts; matching company text and a supplied URL do not independently verify the employer entity or employee authorship. surfaces selects reddit,youtube,news,naver,naver_blog,naver_cafe,linkedin. Explicit linkedin requires linkedin_url. phrases accepts one to three comma-separated workplace phrases, defaulting to working at,work culture,employee experience. timeframe accepts all/day/week/month/year and 7d/30d/365d aliases; optional date_from/date_to apply inclusive UTC calendar-day filters to delivered windows. Relative dates use the signed request start time. Feasible native Reddit timeframe, YouTube publication bounds and indexed-news date filters are forwarded; local filters still bound the delivered windows. The quoted phrase expression is retrieval input, not a guarantee of identical Boolean-search semantics across providers. These are retrieval samples, not complete histories.

Each source provides one bounded window. At most ten Reddit posts are retained and the first two receive a separate top-level comment read; at most ten comments per post are retained with exact parent binding. YouTube and indexed news return up to five snippets. A separate native indexed-news sentiment read preserves provider polarity/emotion count distributions over the filtered index, with internal lists bounded to one. Those NLP counts can overlap and are not employee-authorship proof, human judgments, or the same population as returned snippets. They remain separate from the quote-based model analysis. Each Naver corpus returns up to five records. LinkedIn reads exact company identity before one source page and retains up to ten company-bound posts. Unknown dates remain in locally filtered windows. Day-only Naver dates stay published_date with published_at=null and date_precision=day; no midnight instant is invented. Titles/text are Unicode-safe prefixes of at most 2,000 UTF-8 bytes; text_truncated indicates either prefix was cut. source_rows sums observed rows in successful retrieval/comment windows and excludes native aggregate citation counts; failed-source row counts remain unknown. Source URLs are inert HTTP/HTTPS metadata and are not fetched. No media, author/contact enrichment, continuation or deeper refill is performed.

One optional existing xAI Chat request classifies at most twelve 700-byte text prefixes selected round-robin across source/comment windows. Company, keys, surfaces and text are untrusted data. Labels must cover exact indices and quote the same prefix. Dropped/uncertain records retain unknown context/sentiment/topic. Employee-self-report means a claim expressed in text, not verified employment or truth. Per-surface sentiment uses positive=1,negative=-1,neutral/mixed=0,unknown excluded. Voice gap requires at least three known company-voice and three employee-self-report observations and subtracts their sampled means. Topic memberships, counts and quotes are recomputed in code. These labels and means do not establish working conditions, company reputation or a person verdict. An unavailable model stays null with explicit usage at the same source price.

A 30-credit hold applies. If fewer than half of requested source axes answer, 15 credits are retained; otherwise 30. Optional comment, native aggregate or model failure does not reduce source-axis coverage. Explicit all-source empty windows cost zero, while nonempty source windows retain the applicable price even when local date filters remove every record. All-source failure, caller cancellation (499) and the global deadline (504) fully refund. Immutable replay is free. Independent HMAC request/evidence/time receipts are reprojected at settlement. Source cash remains unknown where no verified conversion exists; a known floor does not become a complete invoice.

The configured global window is 55 seconds, ordinary per-source work is bounded to 18 seconds and the native sentiment read to 24 seconds, and model work has its additional 12-second bound. These are limits, not measured latency guarantees.
