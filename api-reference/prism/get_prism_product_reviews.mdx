Collects bounded Amazon, Google Shopping and Trustpilot review observations. Anchor with asin, google_id or trustpilot_domain, or supply query. The gid compatibility alias means this upstream's documented Google Shopping ID, not a verified Socialcrawl gid/catalogid crosswalk. With query, all three sources are selected by default; otherwise only explicitly anchored sources. Country defaults to US and supports US,GB,CA,DE,FR,IT,ES,JP,IN,MX,BR,AU,NL as requested marketplace codes. Source country availability is unverified; Trustpilot remains a company/service axis without a country filter (product.country=null), not a matching product SKU. country_basis separates native Amazon marketplace echoes, requested/unverified search or Google markets and the unfiltered company axis. depth is 1–100 (default 10), an upper bound within one source window. include defaults to topics,timeline,retailer_matrix; an empty value omits optional blocks. competitors accepts up to two comma-separated queries and adds that block. sellers and social_echo are opt-in.

An unanchored source searches once, retains up to three native candidates and reads the first candidate's product and review windows for main sources. Competitors collect their product/aggregate windows without extra review reads. One optional quote-based model pass may accept, reject or leave that first candidate uncertain. No unjudged first-result fallback, accessory/reseller/variant substitution or verified cross-source identity is inferred. Other candidates are retained for inspection; no deeper candidate walk. Exact anchors bypass semantic matching but retain source identity guards. A missing detail can coexist with a valid request-bound review window; review association is native_id_echo only where the source actually echoes the product ID. Missing review IDs remain null with explicitly local hash/position keys. Duplicate known IDs fail. Source raw dates are preserved; only strict ISO days/instants normalize, with day-only dates retaining published_at=null. Title/text prefixes and truncation are explicit, reviewer/contact/media data omitted. Source credit units are not USD.

The existing pinned US Amazon Actor 0.1.92 review path is preserved when its existing key is configured: at most ten top/featured rows, four-cent full reservation from the unchanged shared Apify500-cent journal. This is source selection, not a retry chain; it is not newest order or full history. Other commerce reads use documented Monocrawl contracts with five-source-credit ceilings (seven for Trustpilot company detail) reserved from the existing zero/unallocated directory journal. Signup remains declined; no account/key was created. A queued descriptor is validated against its exact task ID and owner-scoped path. At most three documented free web/jobs/get status reads (X-Max-Credits:0, 500ms between later reads) can retrieve its completed operation-specific result under the same source deadline. Native job IDs/status and any full result envelope are checked before the original product/review parser. No job list/cancel request, arbitrary returned URL, resubmission or new source-credit reservation. A task still pending remains explicitly UPSTREAM_PENDING. Upstream queued work may continue consuming its capped allowance, without a cancellation/refund guarantee. Native usability/capacity and completed/deferred-task usability remain release qualifications.

Listed five-star aggregates are preferred; otherwise only known five-star sample ratings produce sample means. Native counts and source scales remain separate, unknowns stay null. Trustpilot company ratings are not the same population as product reviews. Timeline and retailer matrix cover these windows only; the pooled sample mean is descriptive across unverified populations, not a global product rating. Competitor axes remain separate. An optional Google Shopping offer window keeps base price, currency, shipping text and unknown total price; no checkout, stock, seller ownership or cross-currency comparability claim. Optional social echo retains one Reddit and one YouTube query window from documented sources, without claiming topic relevance, reviewer equivalence or complete mention counts. No media or arbitrary links are fetched.

One existing xAI Chat request receives at most nine candidate and six round-robin review prefixes, each 700 UTF-8 bytes. Candidate matches and review topics/sentiment require exact indexed same-prefix quotes. These are text hypotheses, not truth or authenticity judgments. Topic memberships/counts, sample means and differences are recomputed in code; rating differences are not causal effects. Model failure leaves explicit-anchor observations intact at the source price and query candidates unresolved; it does not silently accept the first result.
