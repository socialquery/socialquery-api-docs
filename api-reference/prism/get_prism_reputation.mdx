<Note>This partial adapter is staged locally and disabled. One native Spotify company/app pair succeeded. Production remains 2026-10-11.120 pending the combined audit.</Note>

Use a bare company domain in `brand`, explicit `axis=company|product|both`, the matching source set (company: `trustpilot`; product: `app_store`; both: `trustpilot,app_store`), and `include=timeline`. Product/both require an exact numeric `app_store_id`; company forbids it. Country is US (US, United States or 2840); depth is 1–20/default 20. Brand names, URLs, automatic app discovery, place/Google Play, other markets/sources, default include and AI summaries reject before reservation.

The company axis reuses one pinned bounded Trustpilot Actor window. Native company ID/domain/status must agree. Company TrustScore, displayed stars, histogram mean and sample mean remain separate; the displayed company rating prefers TrustScore. The histogram sums to the company corpus count. The public listing/status total binds native review_count_filtered and may differ: Spotify reported corpus 5,876 and listing 5,682. The source does not explain this difference; no language, filter, moderation or removal cause is inferred. A zero listing alone cannot establish no company reviews.

The product axis reads one public Apple software lookup and at most one 50-entry US review feed page. The app ID and storefront must agree, and the listing seller URL hostname must match the requested brand domain. This corroborates a publisher declaration, not independently verified ownership. Spotify's listing reported average 4.77128 from 42,617,924 ratings; this population is distinct from written reviews and company ratings. The listed aggregate is preferred; sample mean is a labeled fallback. All source rows are validated before displaying at most depth rows, preserving native feed positions without claiming global rank, completeness or first-publication order. No continuation is followed or exposed.

Separate timelines use Trustpilot day-level publication dates and Apple UTC feed-entry update days. Apple updated timestamps are explicitly not first-publication times; published_at stays null. Timelines cover bounded, nonrandom windows with different audiences and timescales. They are not blended into a weighted reputation score, comparable sentiment/demand signal or spam conclusion. Overall score, sentiment and AI pros/cons stay null; this adapter makes no model request. Full source coverage and reference scoring methodology remain unverified.

Each requested source has an explicit outcome. A failed Apple review feed retains a valid listing rating with null review/timeline data. A failed company source can retain app data; missing credentials are distinguished from a dispatched paid attempt. All-source failure is an unbilled execution error, never an empty success or absence claim. Known zero-rating evidence refunds all thirty credits. Otherwise the usable selected rating-source ratio retains thirty credits except strictly below one half, which refunds fifteen. With two supported sources one of two is exactly one half and retains thirty; the strict-below-half branch needs wider sources to become reachable. Native negative/refund and full reference pricing parity remain pending.

One source Actor is capped at $0.04, twenty reviews, forty seconds/512 MiB, with a 45-second source deadline. Public Apple reads use no key or paid provider credit. A 55-second common deadline includes reservation, source work and projection; each transport is at most twelve seconds/1 MiB, output 4 MiB and canonical receipts ten minutes. Failed paid work after dispatch leaves provider cost unknown, with a separate known floor; Apify usage is an estimate rather than a final invoice. Credentials stay at their intended origin. Raw reviewer identity/contact/media/replies, seller enrichment and arbitrary tokens are omitted from independent receipts. Runtime cache is zero, source rate two requests/minute, own journal zero/unallocated and all three activation flags false. Broader sources, score/AI/default semantics, native empty/error acceptance, sustainable capacity and combined REST/MCP/Python/ledger audit remain gaps.
