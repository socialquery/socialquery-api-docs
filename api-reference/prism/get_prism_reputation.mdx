Reputation observes six separate public rating/review axes: Trustpilot company domains, Apple App Store and Google Play app listings, Google Business places, TripAdvisor places and the indexed web. Supply brand name or bare domain; axis company/product/both defaults both. Sources default to those axes, with TripAdvisor included only when place or tripadvisor_url is supplied. Optional exact app_store_id, google_play_id, google_place_id and canonical TripAdvisor URL select source listings. Domain brands anchor Trustpilot directly. Name queries retain at most three search candidates per source and read only the first candidate; acceptance requires one exact quoted model hypothesis. Missing inference never silently accepts the first result. Caller anchors select listings without independently verifying brand association. For a domain brand and explicit Apple ID, the native seller-domain must match, preserving the existing gate.

country accepts thirteen requested app storefronts:US,GB,CA,DE,FR,IT,ES,JP,IN,MX,BR,AU,NL; US aliases United States/2840 and UK→GB are accepted. It does not filter reviewer geography, Trustpilot, business places, TripAdvisor or web. place is a search hint, not a geographic filter. depth1–100 defaults20 and is an upper bound: existing Trustpilot Actor20, Apple one native page of at most50, Google Business at most5 most-relevant reviews/no pagination, Google Play/TripAdvisor at most100. A source review is request-bound unless its response corroborates the native target ID. Missing native IDs stay null; local position keys are explicitly local. Raw date text remains visible; only valid ISO days normalize, and Apple RSS updated-entry days are not first-publication dates. No full history, newest/global ranking, reviewer contacts/history, replies, media or linked content is collected. Review-feed failure preserves valid listing aggregates and remains null/unavailable rather than empty. Existing Trustpilot Actor1.0.8 and full four-cent cap/corpus histogram are preserved when configured. Queued TripAdvisor work receives at most three owner-scoped free status reads under the same deadline; no resubmission, arbitrary returned URL, task cancellation or upstream refund assurance.

include defaults themes,timeline,source_matrix; include=timeline retains the earlier supported path and an explicit empty include omits optional computations. One existing xAI Chat request sees at most five candidate plus six round-robin review prefixes, each700 UTF-8 bytes. Exact indexed quotes support entity-match hypotheses and expressed positive/negative/mixed/neutral experience topics. Pros/cons are quote-backed topic groups, never generated factual claims, reviewer truth or authenticity verdicts. Membership and counts are recomputed; only accepted-source review texts can contribute. Optional inference failure preserves anchored ratings and refunds unresolved sources through coverage.

Web evidence makes two bounded DataForSEO requests: universal0–1 rating bins and general indexed-web sentiment without a news/forum page filter. Bin means are interval bounds and midpoint approximations; native NLP citation counts may overlap. Keyword subject identity, completeness and population comparability are unverified. Raw native five-star listing aggregates and bounded sample means remain separate. Zero-count listings are unrated, never a zero reputation score. Source histograms and native TrustScore are distinct.

overall_score aliases our advisory rating_index value, not Socialcrawl's score or a calibrated reputation probability. The published formula is sum(w_i*(rating_i/native_max_i)*100)/sum(w_i) over available accepted ratings only. Both-axis base weights are Trustpilot.25,Apple.20,GooglePlay.20,GoogleBusiness.20,TripAdvisor.10,web.05; company weights.45/.35/.15/.05 for Trustpilot/GoogleBusiness/TripAdvisor/web; product weights.475/.475/.05 for Apple/GooglePlay/web. Available weights renormalize. Listed aggregates take precedence over observed sample means; Trustpilot histogram mean is a separately identified fallback. Web uses the universal-bin midpoint with its mean interval propagated into index bounds. Unknown scores are omitted. These distinct company/app/place/keyword populations are not asserted comparable, independent or the same brand. This is a transparent advisory rating index only; no identity, truth, safety or authenticity conclusion.
